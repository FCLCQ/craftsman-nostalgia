# Resource pack sources and licensing

[中文](https://github.com/FCLCQ/craftsman-nostalgia/blob/main/docs/RESOURCE-PACK-SOURCES.md)

The distribution ships `CNFullTextures-CC.zip`, a redistributable build that bundles `LICENSES/MIT.txt` and `LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`; its `pack.mcmeta` describes it as "工匠怀古全覆盖材质(可分发)".

## Contents

The pack has 3642 entries covering textures and models for `minecraft`, `tconstruct`, `botania`, `twilightforest`, `taiga`, `conarm` and related namespaces. Assets come from ModernityExtra (MIT) and Backport Everything (CC BY-SA 4.0) and are included as-is. Sounds and language files are provided by the mods and vanilla 1.12.2 instead.

Overrides that conflict with Tinkers' Antique 2.13.0.x generated part textures or with Botania's texture domain, such as `assets/tconstruct/textures/items/` and `assets/botania/textures|models|blockstates/`, are excluded.

## License

The pack is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0; MIT assets from ModernityExtra keep their original license, both reproduced under `LICENSES/`. Vanilla Minecraft assets remain copyright Mojang Studios and require attribution and share-alike on redistribution.

## Excluded sources

The FFD additional pack (no license file), Modernity Adjunct (LGPL), Fabric Chests (GPL-3.0) and Modernity-f3 (LGPL-3.0-only) are not included. New assets must have a compatible license and a recorded source and modification scope before inclusion.
